FAQs

Questions
answered.

Have something else on your mind? Reach out any time at oneextracookie@gmail.com.

What is your pricing?
My cookies are priced in three tiers based on design complexity — starting at $4.50 per cookie for simple designs, up to $12.00 for signature artisan work. Minimum order quantities apply; see the Pricing section for the full breakdown.
Do you have availability for my date?
Check my availability calendar above! Since all my orders are custom, I can't confirm your date until I've reviewed your submission. I respond to all requests within 48 hours.
Where are you located?
I'm based in South Olathe, Kansas. All orders are available for local pickup. Shipping is available for customers who cover shipping costs, though local pickup is always preferred for freshness.
