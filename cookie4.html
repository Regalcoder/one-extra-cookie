FAQs

Questions
answered.

What is your pricing?
My cookies are priced in three tiers based on design complexity — starting at $4.50 per cookie for simple designs and up to $12.00 for signature artisan work. Minimum order quantities apply. See the full breakdown in the Pricing section above.
Do you have availability for my date?
You can check my current availability in the calendar above. Keep in mind that all my orders are custom, so I can't confirm your date until I've reviewed your submission. I respond to all inquiries within 48 hours.
Where are you located?
I'm based in South Olathe, Kansas. All orders are available for local pickup. I'm also able to ship for customers willing to cover shipping costs, though local pickup is preferred to ensure your cookies arrive in perfect condition.
